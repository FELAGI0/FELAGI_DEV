A biophysics web app for medical schools: it reads an ECG from a photo, solves
problems step by step and explains phenomena at the level the student asks for —
from first year to exam prep.

## What is inside

- **Six modules** — ECG, problems, explanations, a Nernst calculator, stats and
  history; they switch by tab, each keeping its own state.
- **Anonymous cookie session** — the tool is meant to work without signing up,
  while a JWT attaches history to an account and enables sharing.
- **Rate limit** — 30 AI requests per hour per session, an in-memory window on
  a single uvicorn worker: LLM calls cost money, so the cap sits on the
  endpoints rather than only in the UI.
- **Safety guard** — questions outside medicine and biophysics are rejected on
  the way in.
- **PDF export** through fpdf2 with a Unicode font for Cyrillic, plus a public
  result link by token.
- **Formulas** render with KaTeX — both in LLM answers and in the calculator.

## Stack

FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy 2.0, Alembic and PostgreSQL on the backend; React +
Vite + TypeScript and Tailwind on the frontend. The model is called through an
OpenAI-compatible proxy, and the key lives only in the server-side `.env`.

## Demo

[biophyslab.ru](https://biophyslab.ru) — no sign-up needed; history is tied to
the cookie session.
