A CRM for managing customers and deals: roles and access control, a sales
pipeline with a drag-and-drop kanban board, activity history and reporting.

## What is inside

- **RBAC** — roles and granular permissions enforced at the endpoint level,
  not just hidden in the UI.
- **Async SQLAlchemy 2.0** — fully asynchronous data access, migrations
  managed with Alembic.
- **335 tests** — the API, business logic and access rules are covered by
  pytest.

## Stack

FastAPI on the backend, React + TypeScript on the frontend, PostgreSQL as the
primary store. Everything is packaged with Docker.

## Demo

The frontend runs on Vercel, the backend on Render's free tier — the first
request after idle can take about 30 seconds while the instance wakes up. That
is a limitation of the free plan, not of the application.
