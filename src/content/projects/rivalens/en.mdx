A SaaS for keeping an eye on competitors: on a schedule it downloads their
pages, compares them with earlier snapshots by headings, links and prices, and
writes a short Russian summary of what changed with an LLM.

## What is inside

- **Pipeline** — fetch → extract → diff → summarize. Snapshots and changes are
  stored apart from the summaries, so a diff can be recomputed without calling
  the LLM again.
- **Entity diffs** — comparison over the headings, links and prices that were
  extracted, not over raw HTML; the price chart and change history are built
  from the same rows.
- **Scheduler** — APScheduler inside the FastAPI lifespan, plus a manual
  "Check now" with a cooldown and protection against overlapping runs.
- **Billing** — YooKassa: a plan is raised only by the `payment.succeeded`
  webhook, and the payment status is re-fetched from the API rather than trusted
  from the notification body. Plan limits (free / pro / business) are enforced
  at the endpoint level.
- **SSRF guard** — a competitor accepts only absolute http(s) URLs; loopback and
  private hosts are rejected before the fetcher ever sees them.
- **35 pytest tests** — auth, billing, CRUD, scheduler, pipeline and the guard,
  against a throwaway Postgres via testcontainers.

## Stack

FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + Alembic + PostgreSQL 16 on the backend, Next.js 15 (App
Router, TypeScript) and Tailwind on the frontend. The fetcher is httpx +
BeautifulSoup with no Playwright: the service has to fit in 2 GB of RAM on a
VPS. Docker Compose throughout, nginx and certbot in production.

## Demo

[rivalens.ru](https://rivalens.ru) — the production VPS. Sign-up is required: a
new account is seeded with a demo competitor so it never opens on an empty
screen.
